# Changelog

All notable changes to the App Store Screenshot Generator. The app's "What's New" sheet is
generated from this file, so keep the format: `## [version] - YYYY-MM-DD`, an optional one-line
summary, then `- ` bullets.

## [Unreleased]

Ready-made layouts and precise placement.

- Seven layouts to start from, shown as live previews of your screen: Classic, Big & Bold, Rising, Floating, Tilted Left, Tilted Right and Text Below.
- Text can now sit below the device.
- Fine-tune any layout: position, size, tilt, left/right and up/down are always at hand, the device snaps to the centre while dragging, and the arrow keys nudge it (Shift for bigger steps).
- AI agents can use the same layouts through the MCP server (layoutPreset, textPosition).

## [1.11.0] - 2026-10-11

Easier controls, more devices and more backgrounds.

- New devices: iPhone Duo (open and closed), Mac and Apple Watch, plus the latest iPhone 18 Pro Max and iPad Pro M5 frames.
- Simpler inspector: Text, Background and Device tabs in plain language, with S–L text sizes and automatic readable text colours.
- Screenshots now fit every frame pixel-perfectly: no background showing in the corners (previously visible on iPad) and no screenshot edges outside the device.
- Drag the device on the canvas to move it.
- 24 new gradients chosen for App Store screenshots, organised into Vibrant, Warm, Cool, Soft and Dark.
- Apply a screen's style to every screen in one click.
- "What's New" shows the release history.

## [1.10.0] - 2026-10-11

A cleaner, Apple-style editor.

- Redesigned in the style of a native Mac app: system font, grouped cards, segmented controls and switches, in light and dark mode.
- A clearer start: upload screenshots right from the empty canvas, with the three steps explained.
- The export button now says exactly what it includes, for example "iPhone & iPad · All languages".

## [1.9.0] - 2026-10-11

AI agents can now create your screenshots.

- MCP server: Claude, Cursor and other AI agents can render branded screenshot sets, hosted at screenshots.linkandreas.de/mcp or run locally.
- Choose from eight typefaces for headlines and descriptions.
- New editor design and self-hosted fonts.
